Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Wisely Spent is built on the principle of intentional utility, not data monetization. We aim to be completely transparent about what we collect, why we collect it, and how you stay in control of your information.

This policy applies to The Earn Lens app and to this website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use The Earn Lens, we collect only what is strictly necessary to run your account and calculate your time conversions:

  • Account email: Used exclusively for sign-in, account management, and essential service messages.
  • Financial figures you enter: The hourly wage, income, and purchase amounts you type into the app.

We use this information only to provide the app's features. We do not sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

2. No Bank Connections or Automatic Syncing

The Earn Lens relies entirely on manual entry. We do not integrate with Plaid, banks, card providers, or any other financial data aggregator. You decide which numbers to enter, and we never have access to your bank accounts or payment credentials.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

Lifetime purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your card details. We receive only the information needed to confirm that your purchase is active.

App Store Privacy Summary

For full transparency, here is how our practices map to the Apple App Store and Google Play privacy disclosures:

  • Data linked to you: Contact info (email address) and financial info (the income and purchase figures you enter).
  • Data used to track you: None.
  • Third-party analytics: None.
  • Advertising identifiers: None.

4. Storage and Retention

Your data is stored securely with our hosting provider, which processes it only on our behalf and only to operate the app. We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is permanently removed.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all of your data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In the app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all of your entries from our database.
  • By email: Email privacy@thewiselyspent.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion". We will process your request within 48 hours.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Please cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

6. Third-Party Tracking and Analytics

We do not use advertising trackers, sell data to brokers, or run third-party behavioral analytics in The Earn Lens. Your focus belongs on your choices, not on being monitored.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including in the EU/EEA under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The Earn Lens is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the app.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email privacy@thewiselyspent.com.
