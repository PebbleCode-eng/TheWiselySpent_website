The Earn Lens

Make your time count. Track every income stream, log the hours you work, and see what each hour is really worth, so you can build toward the life you want.

1. Every Income Stream

Add any way you earn: client work, things you're building, or passive income. Give each stream its own goal and watch your progress toward the year.

2. Know What an Hour Is Worth

Every stream shows its true hourly rate, project by project, so you can see which work pays and which pays later, by design.

3. Log Hours in Seconds

Pick the day, choose the income stream, tap in the time. Done. Logging income is just as quick, with no clutter and no aggressive notifications.
