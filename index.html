About Wisely Spent

Wisely Spent is written by one person, not a finance company. I'm not a financial adviser and I'm not selling a course. I just got tired of money advice that was either "retire at 35 on rice and beans" or "treat yourself, you deserve it", and started working out the middle path in public.

That means tracking where my time and money actually go, cutting what doesn't matter, and spending without guilt on what does. I built The Earn Lens because I wanted to know what my hours were actually worth: which work paid well, which paid badly, and which was building toward something. Now the app keeps track of it for me.
