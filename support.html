Help for The Earn Lens: Income Tracker

Contact Us

Email thewiselyspent@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can, usually within two working days.

To help us help you, please include your device model, operating system version, and the app version (found in Settings).

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the "hours of work" figure calculated?

The Earn Lens divides a price by the after-tax hourly income you enter. If your figure looks off, check the wage and income values you entered in Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play and go to Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next year.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used to buy it, then tap Restore Purchases in the app's settings.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Request one through Apple or Google Play.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. The Earn Lens uses manual entry only. It never connects to banks or card providers.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account in the app, or email privacy@thewiselyspent.com from your registered address. See our Privacy Policy for details.
